# High Electricity from Pump — Troubleshooting Guide

## Common in Indian households

Pump sets are among the top home loads. High bills often come from **long run hours**, leaks, low voltage, or an oversized cycle — not only “rate increase.”

## Symptoms

- Bill up but **usage pattern feels the same**.
- Pump audible **many hours daily**, including when tank is full.
- Motor **hot**, trips, or hums — signs of inefficient run.
- Tank overflows or leaks — hidden water waste driving pump time.

## Likely causes

1. **Pump runs too long** — No timer/float; manual forgetfulness.
2. **Overflow or leak** — Pump refills what is lost 24×7.
3. **Low voltage** — Higher current for same work (meter reads more units).
4. **Wrong HP / inefficient pump** — Old or mismatched to depth.
5. **Stuck float or valve** — Continuous or very frequent cycles.
6. **Shared meter confusion** — Actually other appliances increased.
7. **Power factor / old starter** (commercial) — Penalty or loss on some tariffs.

## Quick checks

- Count **how many minutes per day** the pump actually runs (one day log).
- Check **overflow**, terrace wet spots, and toilet leaks.
- Compare bill **units (kWh)** month to month, not only rupees.
- Note if motor runs **after tank is audibly full**.
- Verify **timer setting** matches current fill time.

## Fixes

- Set **timer to actual fill time** minus 2–5 minutes; adjust weekly.
- Repair **float, foot valve, and leaks** first — cheapest saving.
- Run pump in **off-peak hours** if your tariff rewards it (DISCOM rules vary).
- Service pump if flow dropped but run time increased (working harder).
- Ask electrician about **voltage** if lights dim heavily when pump starts.

## Prevention tips

- **Tank-full stop** is the biggest household saving — float + timer backup.
- Teach family the **approved run schedule**.
- Review after **monsoon** (fill time changes).
- Replace **rusted leaking tanks** and pipes that trigger extra cycles.

## When to call a technician

- Suspected **meter fault** — DISCOM complaint process.
- Motor amps **above nameplate** with good water flow — winding or mechanical issue.
- Need **solar / VFD / pump replacement** study — dealer or energy auditor.

## Eshwar recommendation

An **Eshwar auto-cut timer** aligned to real fill time often cuts daily units sharply by stopping at full tank — typical payback story for apartments and farmhouses.

## Safety

Do not open electricity meter seals yourself. Panel changes for tariff or solar must be done by licensed electricians.
