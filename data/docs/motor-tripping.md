# Motor Tripping Repeatedly — Troubleshooting Guide

## Common in Indian homes & farms

When a motor trips the breaker or overload again and again, the cause is often electrical load, water path blockage, or a failing starter component — not just a “bad switch.”

## Symptoms

- Breaker trips **seconds after** start, or after a few minutes of run.
- **Overload relay** clicks off on starter panel (surface pumps).
- Motor starts once, trips on **second start** soon after.
- Tripping worse on **summer evenings** (voltage-related).

## Likely causes

1. **Dry run / low water** — Pump runs light then heavy; current spikes.
2. **Blocked delivery or foot valve** — High load on motor.
3. **Weak bore / air entry** — Irregular load and vibration.
4. **Low or unbalanced voltage** — High current draw.
5. **Wrong overload setting** — Too sensitive for actual running amps.
6. **Damaged cable or joint** — Heat and intermittent trip.
7. **Capacitor / starter fault** (single-phase) — Won’t hold run.
8. **Winding insulation weak** — Trips under load; may smell hot.

## Quick checks

- Confirm **water flow** when motor runs before trip.
- Wait **2–3 minutes** after trip before restart (motor and breaker need cooling).
- Note if trip happens at **start only** vs during steady run.
- Check if **other heavy loads** run on same line (grinder, AC).
- Look for **loose connections** at starter (visual only; don’t touch live parts).

## Fixes

- Restore **water level** in bore; service foot valve and suction leaks.
- Clear **delivery line blockage** and open valves fully.
- Reduce **frequent on–off**; use timer to limit cycles per hour.
- Ask electrician to check **voltage, cable size, overload setting, capacitor**.
- Avoid **manual repeated restarts** when hot — worsens damage.

## Prevention tips

- Use **dry-run protection** where water level varies.
- Keep a **run log**; sudden trips after years often mean bore yield drop or bearing wear.
- Annual **starter and cable inspection** for agricultural motors.
- Match **HP and stages** to current depth — re-evaluate if bore was deepened.

## When to call a technician

- Burning smell, blackened wires, or breaker will not stay on after cooling.
- Tripping continues when water flow is confirmed good.
- Any **panel, capacitor, or winding** work — licensed electrician only.

## Eshwar recommendation

Timer-based **auto-cut** reduces pointless restarts when the tank is already full — fewer start–stop cycles mean fewer trips and less stress on the starter.

## Safety

Never bypass breakers or overloads. Do not touch the starter with wet hands. Children must not operate pump panels.
