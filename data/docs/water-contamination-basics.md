# Water Contamination Basics — Safety Guide

## Common concerns in homes & farms

People worry about smell, colour, worms, or stomach illness. This guide covers **basics only** — not a lab diagnosis. When in doubt, **test water** and stop drinking until cleared.

## Symptoms people notice

- **Rotten egg, chemical, or petrol smell**
- **Persistent colour** (yellow, brown, green tint)
- **Worms or insects** in tank or tap
- **Salty or metallic taste**
- **Family illness** after switching to bore/tank water

## Likely causes (general)

1. **Tank not covered** — Dust, birds, leaves, insects.
2. **Bore intrusion** — Surface pollution near pit, septic influence (area dependent).
3. **Old corroded pipes** — Metal taste; not always bacterial.
4. **Stagnant storage** — Biofilm in unused lines.
5. **Cross-connection** — Garden pipe submerged in tank during refill (back-siphon risk).
6. **Agricultural runoff** — Seasonal nitrate or pesticide risk in some regions.
7. **Flooding** — Contaminated source after heavy rains.

## Quick checks

- Is the **tank lid sealed** and overflow screened?
- When was the tank **last cleaned** properly?
- Any **septic tank, drain, or cattle shed** uphill from bore/tank?
- Did problem start **after rains or plumbing work**?
- Use a **simple test strip or lab** for bacteria/nitrate if illness occurred.

## Fixes (safe household level)

- **Stop drinking** suspect water; use bottled or known-safe source temporarily.
- **Clean and disinfect tank** with approved procedure — empty, scrub, chlorinate, flush fully before use.
- Fix **lid, mesh, and overflow** to block pests.
- Remove **submerged hoses** from tanks; install air gap if possible.
- Flush lines after cleaning; run waste water away from tank inlet.

## Prevention tips

- **Annual tank clean** schedule for drinking lines.
- Keep bore area **free of garbage and chemicals**.
- Separate **drinking vs irrigation** lines where budget allows.
- Educate family: no washing hands over open tank.

## When to call authorities / experts

- **Fuel, sewage, or chemical smell** — stop all use; inform local water/health officials per your state guidance.
- **Outbreak of diarrhea** in household — medical care first, then water test.
- Bore **salinity or arsenic** suspected — only certified lab + government schemes for treatment.

## Eshwar recommendation

Timers do not purify water, but **shorter, controlled pumping** reduces stirring sediment in stressed bores. Pair with filters and testing for drinking lines.

## Safety

Boiling helps many bacteria but **not all chemicals or heavy metals**. Do not rely on colour or “clear” alone. Pregnant women, infants, and elders need confirmed-safe water.
