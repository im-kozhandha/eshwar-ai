# Low Water Pressure — Troubleshooting Guide

## Common in Indian homes & buildings

Weak flow at taps and showers is frustrating in multi-storey houses, farm lines, and ageing GI pipelines. Pressure depends on tank height, pipe size, leaks, and pump health.

## Symptoms

- **Trickle** at upper-floor taps when ground floor is fine.
- Shower feels weak; washing machine fills **very slowly**.
- Pressure OK for **first few minutes**, then drops (leak or tank level).
- Only one line affected (localized blockage or valve).

## Likely causes

1. **Low tank level** — Less head pressure on upper floors.
2. **Undersized pipes** — Long runs with narrow GI lines.
3. **Partially closed valves** — Main gate or terrace valve not fully open.
4. **Choked filters / aerators** — Sediment at tap or inline mesh.
5. **Leaks underground** — Pressure never builds; high water bill possible.
6. **Weak pump or wrong HP** — Cannot push to height or distance.
7. **Air lock in pipes** — After emptying tank or pipeline work.
8. **Shared supply peak load** — Many flats drawing at same time.

## Quick checks

- Check **roof tank level** at the time of weak flow.
- Open and close **main isolation valve** fully once.
- Remove and clean **tap aerators** and inline filters.
- Compare flow at **ground vs top floor** same time.
- Listen for **hissing** near pipes (possible leak).

## Fixes

- Fill tank to **adequate level** before peak morning use.
- Replace or upsize **choked sections** (dealer can suggest pipe dia).
- Clean **foot valve, suction, and pump impeller** if bore flow is weak.
- Bleed **air** from lines after plumbing work (open highest tap carefully).
- Fix **visible leaks**; call plumber for hidden line leaks.

## Prevention tips

- Schedule pump runs so tank is **full before 6–8 AM** usage.
- Install **periodic filter cleaning** in muddy bore areas.
- Avoid running too many **boosters** on one small pump without advice.
- Log pressure complaints by **floor** to pinpoint line vs pump issue.

## When to call a technician

- Pump runs but **no water** at any tap (electrical or bore issue).
- Suspected **underground leak** under road or compound.
- Need to change **pump HP, stages, or pipeline layout** for building height.
