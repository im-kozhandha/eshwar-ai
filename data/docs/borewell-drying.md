# Borewell Drying — Troubleshooting Guide

## Common in farms & water-scarce areas

A bore “drying” means yield dropped — not always that the aquifer is gone. Season, pump depth, and over-pumping often look like a dead bore before it truly is.

## Symptoms

- Pump runs but **very little or no water** at outlet.
- Long run times needed to **fill the same tank** as last year.
- **Air gurgling** in pipes; pump sounds laboured.
- Neighbours report **similar problems** in dry months.

## Likely causes

1. **Seasonal water table drop** — Summer dip is common.
2. **Pump set above water level** — Needs lowering or smaller stages adjustment.
3. **Over-pumping** — Running 24×7 faster than aquifer recharge.
4. **Partial clogging of screen** — Less intake, feels like dry bore.
5. **Leaking pipes** — Water lost before reaching tank.
6. **Motor running dry** — Damaged impeller; flow looks like “no water.”
7. **Multiple bores nearby** — Shared aquifer stress.

## Quick checks

- Compare **fill time now vs last season** (same timer setting).
- Note **time of day** yield is better (night recharge in some areas).
- Check for **leaks** on delivery and tank inlet.
- Listen for **air** vs steady water sound at pump.
- Ask neighbours if **area-wide** shortage this month.

## Fixes

- **Reduce daily run hours**; give aquifer recovery time.
- Consult dealer about **lowering pump** or yield test (not DIY).
- **Clean or develop** bore if silt choked — licensed bore service only.
- Fix **leaks and valves** before assuming aquifer failure.
- Store water in tank during **better yield hours** if known.

## Prevention tips

- Track **litres or tank fills per week** — early warning of decline.
- Avoid **24×7** unless designed for your yield test results.
- Rainwater recharge pits where feasible (local NGO / agri guidance).
- Plan **backup source** (tanker, secondary bore) before peak summer.

## When to call a technician

- Electrical tripping with **no flow** — may be dry-run damage, not only dry bore.
- Need **submersible pull, camera logging, or hydro-fracturing** — specialists only.
- Water quality turned **salty or smelly** suddenly — different problem, test water.

## Eshwar recommendation

An Eshwar **auto-cut timer** caps daily run time so the bore is not pumped empty every day — especially helpful when yield is already falling.

## Safety

Do not keep restarting a submersible that may be running dry — risk of motor burn. Do not drink less volume water without quality check if salinity is suspected.
