# Eshwar Auto-Cut Timer — Setup & Use Guide

## What it does

An **auto-cut timer** stops the pump after a set duration — so the tank does not overflow and the motor does not run unnecessarily. It is a simple guard for households, farms, and small buildings.

## Typical symptoms it helps

- Tank **overflows** when nobody switches off.
- Pump runs **all night** after forgetfulness.
- **High electricity** from extra run hours.
- **Motor heat / trips** from too many cycles when combined with a float.

## Who it suits

- Single-phase and three-phase pump lines (model-dependent — check label).
- Roof tanks with **fairly stable fill time**.
- Sites where a **float is unreliable** or needs a backup.

## Quick checks before buying

- Note **minutes to fill tank** from empty to audible full (average 3 days).
- Know **motor HP and phase** (single / three).
- Confirm **starter type** — timer usually wired in control path per local practice.
- Check if a **float already stops** the pump — timer can be backup, not duplicate conflict.

## Setup basics (conceptual)

1. Set duration **slightly below** normal fill time (e.g. fills in 22 min → start at 20 min).
2. Adjust **one minute at a time** over a week until overflow stops.
3. If water still low in tank, add **1–2 minutes** — small steps only.
4. Use **manual override** only when needed; return to auto mode after.

## Fixes for common issues

- **Still overflows** — Timer too long; reduce minutes or service float.
- **Tank not full** — Timer too short; increase slightly or check bore yield.
- **Pump does not start** — Electrical issue separate from timer; call electrician.
- **Frequent trips** — See motor tripping guide; do not only increase timer.

## Prevention tips

- Re-check setting after **season change** or new family usage.
- Write the **chosen minutes** on the panel door.
- Pair timer + **good float** for apartments.
- Teach all family members **not to bypass** without reason.

## When to call a technician

- New **three-phase panel** wiring or unclear neutral/phase routing.
- Burning smell from timer or contactor.
- Pump amp draw **above normal** — mechanical/electrical issue, not timer length alone.

## Eshwar product note

Eshwar auto-cut timers are built for **daily pump duty** in Indian voltage conditions. Your dealer can match model to HP, phase, and whether you need **dual backup with float**. This is operational hardware — not a substitute for water testing or electrical safety devices.

## Safety

Installation must follow **local electrical code**. Timer does not replace **overload protection, earthing, or dry-run devices** where required. Never bypass breakers. Keep panels closed and dry.
